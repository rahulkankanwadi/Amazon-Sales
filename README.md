## Purpose 

This dashboard aims to provide a comprehensive overview of Amazon sales performance, enabling stakeholders to: 

* Track overall sales and identify top-performing categories. 

* Monitor order fulfillment status and identify potential bottlenecks. 

* Analyze geographical sales distribution to understand regional strengths.

 * Evaluate the performance of individual products based on sales, units sold, and returns. 

* Identify sales trends over time.
